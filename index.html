No spare change? Here's how you can help.

Real needs from NYC organizations serving homeless New Yorkers, checked every month.

Pick a way to help, find a place near you, and sign up with them directly.

See someone who needs help right now?

If someone is in danger, on the subway tracks, or needs medical help, call 911.

Otherwise, call 311 or use the 311 app and ask for homeless outreach. The City says its outreach teams try to reach the person within an average of one hour.

In extreme cold the City declares a Code Blue. From 4 pm to 8 am during one, 311 sends these requests straight to 911.

Outreach workers offer shelter and services. Accepting them is the person's choice.

Sources: NYC311 (opens in a new tab), the NYC Department of Homeless Services (opens in a new tab) and the City's HOME-STAT page (opens in a new tab).

    How this works

    The information here comes from each organization, and every listing is checked once a month. Each one shows the date it was last checked.

    Not yet confirmed means the details were taken from the organization's website and the organization hasn't confirmed this month's needs with us yet.

    May be out of date means it has been more than 45 days since the listing was checked. Listings not checked for more than 60 days are taken down until they are checked again.

    Always confirm with the organization before you go. You sign up with them, not with this site.
